# Potion Forager art drafts

## Connected terrain tileset

**[`woodland-tileset/`](woodland-tileset/README.md)** contains the original 32×32 map-building set: grass, dirt, stone, gravel, sand, water, wood, and concrete. Includes connected terrain shapes, three texture alternatives per shape, 12 interior variations per surface, and 12 precomposed material combinations.

- [Surface variations](woodland-tileset/previews/surface_variations.png)
- [Mixed surfaces](woodland-tileset/previews/mixed_surfaces.png)
- [Example woodland map](woodland-tileset/previews/woodland_map.png)
- Godot TileSet: `res://assets/tilesets/woodland/woodland_tileset.tres`
- Editable example: `res://assets/tilesets/woodland/woodland_example.tscn`

## Current character art: imported adventurer adaptations

**Use [`adventurers-spooky-cute/`](adventurers-spooky-cute/README.md)** for the supplied adventurer characters, subtly recolored for the woodland palette, with matching waist-lantern variants. Includes source-compatible 48×64-cell sheets and lossless 32×32 idle/walk exports.

- [Original / recolored / lantern comparison](adventurers-spooky-cute/comparison.png)
- [Lantern walking preview](adventurers-spooky-cute/previews/lantern_walk.gif)
- [Lantern idle preview](adventurers-spooky-cute/previews/lantern_idle.gif)
- [32×32 sprite exports](adventurers-spooky-cute/32x32/)

The supplied originals are in `The Female Adventurer - Free/` and `The Male adventurer - Free/`. Attribution details are recorded in the repository-root `CREDITS.txt`.
